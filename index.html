Afrocan price alerts

Alerts on this phone when a price changes, to print a new tag

Open this from the store report

On this phone, open the Afrocan store report, scroll to the bottom and tap Turn on price alerts on this phone. That brings you back here, ready to go.

Opened from the Home Screen and it still says this? In Safari, on the page that link opened, tap Copy alert link, then come back here and tap Paste.

One step first: add this page to your Home Screen

iPhones only show alerts from pages on the Home Screen.

  1. Tap the Share button ⇧ (bottom of Safari, or next to the address).
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Open Afrocan Alerts from your Home Screen and turn the alerts on there.

If the Home Screen app asks for the alert link, come back here, tap this button, and paste it there.
